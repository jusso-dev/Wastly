# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Parents and carers recording what a child ate and left, often one-handed during or just after a meal. They need fast, private recall rather than nutrition coaching.

## Product Purpose

Wastly is an offline-first child food diary. Success means a carer can log an honest eaten and wasted amount in seconds, find it later, and understand simple patterns without creating an account or sending child details away.

## Brand Personality

Calm, private, practical. Copy is plain Australian English with gentle humour only where it helps perspective.

## Anti-references

Gym-style dark dashboards, Cal AI intensity, medical scoring, BMI lectures, calorie-shaming, noisy gamification, emoji-led controls, and generic analytics-card grids.

## Design Principles

- Make the next logging action obvious.
- Keep child data local by default and explain every optional network boundary.
- Show eaten and wasted amounts without judgement.
- Prefer familiar native controls over novel interaction.
- Keep totals stable, readable, and comparable at a glance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve Dynamic Type and VoiceOver semantics, maintain readable contrast, never rely on colour alone, keep tap targets at least 44 points, and respect reduced-motion settings. Copy must work for varied family structures and avoid clinical judgement.
